05 — About

Thirty years of
workmanship
that lasts.

Founded in 1995 under the leadership of Mr. Y. Khunoo, Austek Engineering CC is a black-owned South African engineering company specialising in boilermaking, pipefitting, and all types of welding and steel fabrication.

Over the past three decades we have delivered reliable, safety-driven solutions across the industrial, commercial, agricultural and residential sectors. Our team is made up of highly qualified boilermakers, welders and fitters who bring technical skill and precision to every project.

With over 20 years of subcontracting experience at Heineken, we have proven our ability to meet strict quality and safety standards inside high-performance production environments — where hygiene, quality and uptime are critical.
